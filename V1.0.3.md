# Trove v1.0.3 — Payment & Order Recovery

Implemented:
- payment intent domain with explicit lifecycle;
- demo payment adapter that never handles card data;
- idempotent payment intent creation per booking;
- payment expiry and rate-limited confirmation;
- reconciliation endpoint for ambiguous outcomes;
- payment event history;
- booking confirmation now requires `paymentStatus=paid` instead of marking payment as paid internally;
- separation between payment success and provider booking;
- provider failure remains recoverable through booking lifecycle events.

Flow:
`preview -> payment intent -> payment success -> provider booking -> confirmed`

Production follow-up:
- hosted/tokenized payment gateway;
- signed webhooks + webhook idempotency;
- persistent payment ledger in PostgreSQL;
- payment/provider reconciliation worker;
- refund/dispute model;
- automatic recovery jobs and operational alerts.
